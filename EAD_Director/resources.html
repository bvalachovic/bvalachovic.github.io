📋 LDA Platform — Sustainment Risk Statement

A governance masterclass. Shows how to make visible the cost of operating a system after launch, demand explicit leadership decisions, and prevent the default path of "unfunded platform accumulation." This is your evidence that director-level thinking is about what happens after ship.

  • The evidence: Concrete timeline of system degradation with zero sustainment funding
  • The decision gate: Three options (fund, launch unfunded accepting risk, defer) with no default option
  • The proof: SAA already has 60+ unfunded platforms accumulating — this is not hypothetical

How to Use These Resources

In the interview: Start with your cover letter to establish operating level. Move to the Car/Engine metaphor when explaining fragmentation. Use the "Identity as a Capability" paper as your strongest evidence that you understand the domain problem. Reference the LDA risk statement as proof that director-level thinking is about sustainability, not just launch.

For architecture questions: Pull the identity ERD diagram, the ABAC overview, and the transitional architecture document. These show you can speak both leadership language (capability model, governance) and technical language (ABAC, claims, SCIM).

For organizational/culture questions: The Car Factory metaphor and your diagnosis section (from the main interview site) explain why speed without architecture creates technical debt and burnout. The LDA risk statement shows how to make decisions visible.

For "tell me about a time" stories: Your cover letter contains four concrete examples: cutting disclosures 45% → 5%, building design systems that drive velocity, the Okta/identity work, and identifying security vulnerabilities. Expand each from the artifacts as needed.
